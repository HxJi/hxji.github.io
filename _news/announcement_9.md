Our paper **Re-architecting End-host Networking with CXL: Coherence, Memory, and Offloading** has been accepted to **MICRO 2025**! This work pioneers the use of a CXL Type-2 device to build end-host networking, leveraging its cache coherence, memory expansion, and offloading capabilities.
