I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [Siebel School of Computing and Data Science](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/) at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champagin](https://illinois.edu/), advised by [Prof. Nam Sung Kim](https://fast.ece.illinois.edu/member.html). I am affiliated with the [FAST](https://fast.ece.illinois.edu/) research group and  Coordinated Science Lab. My research interests include computer architecture, memory systems, computer express link (CXL), and system for ML. I am currently working on CXL Type-2 device to boost hyperscaler application performance.

Before joining UIUC, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering and Finance from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/). I have worked as a visiting researcher at [Meta](https://about.meta.com/) and as a research intern at [Luminous Computing](https://www.luminous.com/) and [Intel Lab](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/research/overview.html).
