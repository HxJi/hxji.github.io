I am a Ph.D. candidate in [Computer Science](https://cs.illinois.edu/) department at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champagin](https://illinois.edu/), advised by [Professor Nam Sung Kim](https://www.cloudbees.com/blog/yaml-tutorial-everything-you-need-get-started). I am affiliated with the [FAST](https://fast.ece.illinois.edu/) research group. My research interests include computer architecture, memory systems, computer express link (CXL), and system for ML. I am currently working on the project of offloading memory-intensive kernel features to CXL Type-2 device.

Before joining UIUC, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/). I have worked as a visiting researcher at [Meta](https://about.meta.com/) and as a research intern in [Luminous Computing](https://www.luminous.com/) and [Intel Lab](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/research/overview.html).
